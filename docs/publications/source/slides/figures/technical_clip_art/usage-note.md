# ContextLens technical clip art

This set supplies five original, editable SVG illustrations for the ContextLens presentation. `project-bridge.svg` is the hero asset: it communicates that StableTradeAtlas and ContextLens are related but distinct projects. The other four assets reinforce dated place identity, retained ambiguity, evidence auditing, and an explicit research boundary.

## Slide mapping

| Asset | Intended slide | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `archive-place.svg` | Why historical addresses are difficult to verify | Introduces the time-dependent place record |
| `ambiguity-branches.svg` | Explicit ambiguity and unresolved states | Reinforces retained competing candidates |
| `audit-ledger.svg` | Implementation and verification | Marks the interpretation as an audit statement |
| `scope-boundary.svg` | Scope and evaluation agenda | Marks the supported-scope boundary |
| `project-bridge.svg` | Acknowledgments | Separates the contest project from the later ContextLens deliverable while showing their relationship |

The SVG files are the editable masters. Matching `*-preview.png` files are transparent 1024 × 1024 previews. `contact-sheet.png` shows the complete family at equal optical scale.

The SVG files remain the editable masters. The deck uses matching PDF vector proxies through standard LaTeX `\includegraphics`, so the documented XeLaTeX workflow does not require shell escape. All assets assume a light background and should retain at least 12% clear space.

These illustrations are conceptual only. They do not encode empirical results, accuracy, causal effects, certification, ownership, or development chronology beyond the adjacent editable slide text.

## Title-icon extension

The expanded title system adds sixteen slide-specific assets documented in `title-icons-manifest.json` and reviewed together in `title-icons-contact-sheet.png`. ContextLens titles use the navy, cobalt, teal, and muted-orange family inside white title badges. StableTradeAtlas appendix headings use a separate purple, green, gold, and dark-ink family so the appendix remains visibly related but distinct.

The title slide uses `context-lens-hero.svg` as a larger anchor illustration. Slides 2 through 12 and the references slide use compact semantic title icons. The appendix overview uses `trade-atlas.svg`, and each team or adviser heading uses an icon matched to that person’s documented role.
